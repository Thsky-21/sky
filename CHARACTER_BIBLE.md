# CHARACTER BIBLE — DID WE MAKE IT?

Two characters, **one person**: Sky at about 19 (First-year, ~2024) and Sky at about 21 (Third-year, ~2026), both
MSRIT students. From PLAN §4–§6 and SCRIPT_TIMELINE, drawn with the `cel-anime-80s` construction (STYLE §2).

> They're about two years apart and must read instantly as the same young man. Third-year Sky never has wrinkles,
> grey hair, a mature beard, a heavier body or a deeper voice; First-year Sky is never childlike. If a model sheet
> makes either one read outside 19–21, stop and redraw.

**About the real appearance:** facial details, skin tone, hairstyle and build below are a **proposed design**,
marked *(proposed)*. They should be replaced by Sky's real features (a few reference photos, front and side, are
enough) before the model sheets are approved. Nothing here claims to be how Sky actually looks.

---

## 1. First-year Sky (~19, asks the questions)

- First year at MSRIT, newly arrived in Bengaluru from Gokak; quiet, a little shy, hopeful, eager to know what
  happened to the dream.
- Sits forward, leans in, reacts quickly, gestures more; eyes a touch brighter.
- Arc: curiosity → excitement → confusion → disappointment → a vulnerable question → quiet determination ("Let's go.").
- He asks; he doesn't explain.

## 2. Third-year Sky (~21, answers)

- Third year at MSRIT, the current Sky; knows what happened, so he's more direct and grounded, sometimes dry.
- Contained, sits back, elbows near the desk; sometimes looks away or down at the page before admitting something.
- Arc: guarded and matter-of-fact → softer → empathetic. The one large physical change: he **leans forward into the
  lamp at 169 s**, the first time he moves toward his younger self.
- Not cruel, cynical, bitter or swaggering; no fake confidence.

## 3. Exact similarities and differences

**Identical (one head function and one body rig, same parameters, shared by both):**
face geometry (head shape, jaw, chin, cheekbones), eye shape, size and spacing, iris colour, brow shape, nose,
mouth, ears, skin colour in every lighting set, hair colour and hairline, height, shoulder width, hands, build.

**Different (only these):**

Never different: age markers (lines, beard, grey, weight), face proportions, height, voice pitch.

## 4. Clothing

Believable for MSRIT students; no suits, luxury watches, founder clichés or college logos (unless supplied and
approved). Both: jeans or plain trousers, simple sneakers.

- **First-year Sky:** plain warm-ivory or light grey crew-neck T-shirt; an unzipped light slate hoodie in the memory
  scenes; navy backpack with a **muted-amber strap**; an amber first-year lanyard (blank card, no text or logo).
  Cel: flat fills, one hard shadow shape, coloured outline (dark version of each fill).
- **Third-year Sky:** a dark ink/slate overshirt worn open over a dark T-shirt, sleeves pushed up; small **muted-teal**
  detail (pocket stitching or the notebook's cover). Slightly creased: an extra shadow fold at the elbows and hem.
  No jewellery beyond what Sky really wears `[FILL]`.

## 5. Face structure (80s cel construction)

- Head built from a sphere plus jaw: a **long egg-shaped face, soft-point chin**; in ¾ the far eye foreshortens and
  the cheekbone turns.
- **Almond eyes** near the vertical middle of the head; a thick upper lash line flicking outward; a tall oval layered
  iris *(proposed: dark brown)* with two highlights. No moe eyes, no oversized irises.
- **Long thin brows**, the main emotion carriers together with lid height.
- Nose: one shadow plane plus one stroke. Mouth: small; 3–4 lip-flap shapes.
- Skin *(proposed: warm medium brown, own colour per lighting set)*; one faint blush layer, never hatched. Skin line
  colour: thin warm dark brown.
- Young adult proportions: no baby-face roundness, no adult hollowing under the cheekbones.
- **No glasses and no stubble or facial hair on either Sky** (Sky's decision, 2026-11-04). Nose: a soft, rounded
  shape (curved shadow plane + a curved base stroke), never pointed.

## 6. Hairstyle (from Sky's reference, `assets/Screenshot 2026-11-04 075435.png`: hair shape only)

- Short, **wavy and smooth** hair (not curly), worn as a **soft curtain fringe**: a centre part, waves sweeping out
  to both sides of the forehead with smooth, slightly spiked tips that open toward the temples, ending just above the
  brows; a little lift at the front crown in profile. Rear view LOCKED (approved 2026-11-04): tapered sides with the ears half showing, a short tapered nape. Very dark brown (reads near-black at night).
- Drawn as a darker hair mass with a smooth edge, then a few long, broad **wave locks** flowing from the crown in gentle
  S-curves (flat fill, hard shadow crescent, thin line), the gaps reading as the dark under-layer, and one continuous
  angel-ring sheen band. Hair line colour: dark purple-brown.
- First-year: the same cut, a little shorter and neater.
- Third-year: the same cut, a few weeks longer: the wave tips a little longer and two loose waves over the brow; same hairline.
- Secondary motion: hair locks as ribbons on a slow travelling sine (6-drawing cycle when calm), lifted slightly by
  the fan in the night room.

## 7. Body language

| | First-year Sky | Third-year Sky |
|---|---|---|
| Seated default | on the front edge of the chair, forearms on the desk, leaning in | back in the chair, elbows near the desk, hands loosely together |
| Listening | eyes on the other's face, small nods, quick shifts | eyes often on the page or the laptop |
| Reacting | fast turns (82 s), raised brows, small smile | a slower glance, a breath before speaking |
| Hands | gestures while speaking (Y03), turns the paper over (Y02) | slides the paper (T01), turns the laptop away (~147 s) |
| Hard moments | looks down at his hands (164 s) | looks away before an admission (20, 126, 156 s) |
| Turning point | — | leans forward into the lamp, looks straight at him (169 s) |
| Ending | stands first, reaches the door | stands, follows him |

Every meaningful move has anticipation, action and follow-through. Characters on twos; blinks, lip flaps and gestures
on threes. Held props sit between the palms; the paper hand-over interpolates from Third-year's palm to First-year's.

## 8. Voice characteristics

Temporary TTS sets timing only; **Sky will record both parts himself** (PLAN §5, `VOICE_REPLACEMENT.md`).

- **Shared:** one young Indian male voice, natural conversational English, light Bengaluru/Karnataka influence; the
  same vocal identity for both. No caricatured accent, no announcer, trailer or motivational delivery. No forced
  slang. No synthetic imitation of any real person.
- **First-year:** ~19; curious, hopeful, slightly shy at first, quicker, occasionally excited; never a childish pitch.
- **Third-year:** ~21; slightly steadier and more measured; direct, guarded and mildly dry at first, warm and
  empathetic near the end; **not deeper**. The difference comes from performance, not pitch.
- Keep breaths, small pauses and imperfect rhythm. Key pauses: after "I don't know how to write the ending yet",
  "Zero replies" and "Zero."
- Mouths move only for on-camera lines; V.O. lines: T03, T04, T05, T06, T07, T11.

## 11. Model sheets to make (with the real drawing code)

1. Per character: turnaround (front, ¾, side), 2–3 key poses, palette swatches per lighting set.
2. Comparison sheet: both heads side by side and overlaid at 50 % (front and ¾); geometry must line up exactly.
3. Expression sheet: the 8 expressions above, on both faces.
4. Arm/shoulder action test: reach, slide the paper, turn the laptop, stand up.

## 12. Never

- A middle-aged, 40-year-old or visibly older Third-year Sky; a childlike First-year Sky.
- Any difference in face geometry, eyes, nose, jaw, skin tone or height between them.
- Elon Musk's likeness, company or college logos (unless approved), identifiable friends, real names or emails.
- Swagger, suits, luxury items, or any visual claim of success, revenue or customers.
